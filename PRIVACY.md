# Sidebar — Privacy Policy

_Effective 13 November 2026_

Sidebar is a Chrome extension that shows your Favorites, pinned tabs, folders and open tabs in Chrome's side panel.

## The short version

Sidebar keeps everything on your computer. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and it never sends your data anywhere.

## What Sidebar stores, and where

Sidebar stores the following **only in your Chrome profile on this device**, using Chrome's extension storage:

- **Your sidebar:** the titles and web addresses of your Favorites and pinned items, your folders and their order, and which folders are collapsed.
- **Your theme color**, if you choose one.
- **Which open tab belongs to which item.** This is temporary: Chrome clears it when the browser restarts.

Chrome deletes all of this when you remove the extension.

## What Sidebar reads

- **Your open tabs** (address, title and icon), to list them in the side panel and to link them to your saved items. This information is used on the spot and is never sent anywhere.
- **Site icons**, through Chrome's built-in favicon service, which reads icons Chrome has already saved on your device.
- **Files you choose yourself**, and only when you choose them: an Arc sidebar file (`StorableSidebar.json`) for the one-time import, or a Sidebar backup file for a restore. Sidebar reads only Favorites, pinned items, folders and the Space color from an Arc file. It never sees any other file.

## What Sidebar does not do

- It does not send data to the developer or to anyone else. It makes no network requests at all.
- It does not read or change the content of web pages, and it does not run scripts on them.
- It does not track your browsing, build profiles or use your data for advertising.
- It does not sell or share data. There is nothing to sell: the developer never receives any.
- It does not use Chrome Sync. Your sidebar stays on the device where you set it up.

## Backups

**Export** on the settings page saves your sidebar and theme color to a file in your Downloads folder. That file is yours: Sidebar does not upload it, and what happens to it afterwards is up to you.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
| --- | --- |
| `sidePanel` | To show the sidebar in Chrome's side panel. |
| `tabs` | To list your open tabs, open and switch to tabs when you click an item, and read a tab's address and title when you save it. |
| `storage` | To save your sidebar and theme color on this device. |
| `favicon` | To show site icons from Chrome's own icon cache. |

## Children

Sidebar is a general-purpose browser tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. A version that would let data leave your device would be a fundamental change. It would be announced in the extension's store listing before it shipped.
